Drive a cherry-red, compact-SUV placeholder along OpenStreetMap road geometry in southern Chennai, with over 20 named interior streets. Widths, building facades and elevations are simplified; this is not a navigation app. Complex interchange ramps are outside the playable route. No Volkswagen model or branding is included.

Original geometry and synthesized three-cylinder engine audio. Six-speed automatic, 120 km/h normal limit and 180 km/h turbo limit. Boost lasts up to 8 seconds and recharges fully in 16 seconds off boost. After emptying, release the boost button to rearm. Night mode adds original neon lighting; traffic follows signals and pedestrians use sidewalks and crossings. Trees are solid; lamp posts can be knocked down. Sound starts with Play; the Sound menu groups engine, music, city ambience and mute controls. All music and sound are original synthesis, not Volkswagen recordings. Street shop locations use available OSM points with small collision-safe facade setbacks; other verified tenants are shown inside the mall. Facades are stylized. No affiliation or endorsement. Renderer: Three.js (MIT). Physics: cannon-es (MIT). Road data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Download the road dataset. No map API key, account or external tile streaming required.
